Privacy policy

Your data stays on your phone.

In short

  • The game collects no personal data and sends nothing anywhere.
  • It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • It does not have Android's permission to use the internet.
  • Your results and settings are kept on your device only.

What the game keeps on your device

So that you can go on where you stopped, the game keeps a few small files in its own storage on your device:

  • your best results in the Campaign and in the Challenges;
  • the settings of Free Play as you left them;
  • the theme you chose.

None of this says who you are, and none of it leaves your device through the game. I cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in the settings of Android.

Backup by Android

If you have switched on the backup of your device to your Google account, Android may include the files above in that backup, so that they are there again on a new phone. Google does this as part of Android, under Google's privacy policy. I have no access to these backups. You can switch the backup off in the settings of your device.

Links out of the game

The settings of the game link to the start page of this site, which leads to this page and to the legal notice. The link opens the browser of your device; the game itself transmits nothing.

If you write to me

When you send me an e-mail I receive your address and what you wrote. I use both only to answer you and delete the mail when the matter is settled, unless the law obliges me to keep it. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

Google Play

You install the game through Google Play. Google processes data there as the operator of the store, under its own privacy policy. From Google I see only figures that name nobody, such as the number of installs and, where a user has agreed to share them with Google, reports of crashes.
